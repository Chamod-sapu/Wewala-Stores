How to Activate Live Money Deduction for Your Shop

Follow these 3 steps to start accepting real money:

Step 1: Create a PayHere Merchant Account
Go to https://www.payhere.lk/merchant/register
 and register an account for the shop.
Submit your basic verification details:
For registered businesses: Business Registration (BR) certificate.
For individual / sole proprietorships: National Identity Card (NIC) & bank account statement.
Link the shop's local bank account (e.g., Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank, HNB, BOC, etc.) where funds should be deposited.



Step 2: Obtain Your Live Credentials
Log in to the PayHere Merchant Portal
.
Go to Settings → Domains & Credentials.
Copy your:
Merchant ID (e.g., 1224567)
Merchant Secret (a 32-character code)



Step 3: Put Credentials into Your .env File

Open apps/web/.env and fill in your live credentials:

env
# Live Payment Gateway Settings
VITE_PAYMENT_GATEWAY=payhere
VITE_PAYHERE_MERCHANT_ID=your_real_merchant_id_here
VITE_PAYHERE_SECRET=your_real_merchant_secret_here
VITE_PAYHERE_SANDBOX=false

IMPORTANT

When VITE_PAYHERE_SANDBOX=false, payments are 100% LIVE: customer debit cards will be debited with real rupees, and the money will be deposited directly into your shop's bank account. If VITE_PAYHERE_MERCHANT_ID is left empty, the system automatically falls back to demo/test mode so development is never blocked.
